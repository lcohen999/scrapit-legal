ScrapIT Software apps — Privacy Policy

IYB Consultants Inc., operating as ScrapIT Software · Effective 26 November 2026

This policy covers ScrapIT Software's mobile apps:

  • ScrapIT Dispatch: for truck drivers at scrap metal, recycling and waste hauling companies;
  • ScrapIT Software: the ScrapIT Touchscreen and ScrapIT GUI for scrap and recycling yards;
  • ScrapIT Agent: connects a yard's printers, scales and cameras to its ScrapIT server.

It explains what information each app handles, why, and who is responsible for it.

Who is responsible for your data

These apps are not consumer services. Each one connects to a ScrapIT system operated by or for your employer: the yard or hauling company that gave you your sign-in. That company decides what data is collected through its operation and how long it is kept; it is the controller of that data. IYB Consultants Inc. (ScrapIT Software) processes the data on that company's behalf in order to provide the software.

If you want work data corrected or deleted, contact your company (your dispatch office or yard administrator): the data lives in your company's ScrapIT system, and your company directs what happens to it. We support those requests when your company passes them to us.

ScrapIT Dispatch

ScrapIT Dispatch is a business tool used by truck drivers to receive and complete the work their dispatch office assigns them.

Information the app collects

  • Driver identity. Your driver code and name, as set up by your company, are used to sign you in and to attribute the work you complete.
  • Precise location. While you are signed in, the app reports the vehicle's position so your dispatch office can see where trucks are, measure the distance to each stop, and order the day's work. It also records a GPS trail against each slip so arrival and departure times are accurate. If you grant background location, this continues while the app is not on screen; if you decline, the app remains fully usable and only automatic arrival detection stops.
  • Photos and camera. The camera is used to scan container barcodes and to photograph bins, sites and paperwork. Photos you capture or attach are uploaded to your company's ScrapIT system as part of the slip record.
  • Signatures and notes. Customer signatures captured on screen and any notes you type become part of the slip record in your company's system.
  • Container and bin data. Bin numbers you scan or enter, and the drop, pickup or reject actions you record.
  • Diagnostics. If the app encounters an error, technical details of the error, your device's browser identification, and its network address are reported to the server so problems can be found and fixed. This is used only for troubleshooting.

Storage on your device

ScrapIT Dispatch is built to keep working where trucks lose signal. To do that, it stores a copy of your assigned slips on the device, and while you are offline it queues the actions you take — container logs, photos, signatures, notes — on the device until a connection returns, at which point they sync to your company's system automatically. If you choose "Remember me", your driver code is stored on the device so you can sign in faster. This locally stored work data is a working copy: its permanent home is your company's ScrapIT system, and uninstalling the app removes the local copy.

Sharing with service providers

To draw maps and plan routes, the app and its server use mapping services: map imagery is loaded from OpenStreetMap tile servers, and street addresses are converted to map coordinates using a geocoding provider. These providers receive only the technical data needed for the request (such as coordinates, an address to look up, and your device's network address), and route calculations for ordering stops are performed using a directions service. No other third parties receive data from the app, except where the law requires disclosure.

ScrapIT Software (Touchscreen and GUI)

The ScrapIT Software app opens your company's ScrapIT Touchscreen (scale tickets, shipping, inventory) and ScrapIT GUI (the office system) from your company's own ScrapIT server.

Information the app handles

  • Server address and sign-in. The server address you enter (or that your IT department sets through device management) and your sign-in are stored on the device so the app can reopen your company's system. Your user name and password are checked by your company's server.
  • Camera and barcode scanning. Photos you take (loads, vehicles, licences and documents) and codes you scan are sent to your company's ScrapIT system as part of the ticket or record you are working on.
  • Microphone. Used only when you speak to Gizmo, the ScrapIT assistant, or dictate into a field; what you say is sent to your company's ScrapIT system to answer you.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, the app gets a device token from Apple or Google and gives it to your company's server, which uses it (through ScrapIT Software's notification service) to send you messages such as chat mentions and alerts. The notification service passes only the token and the short message to Apple or Google for delivery.

Everything else you see and enter in the app is your company's ScrapIT system itself; the app stores no copy of it beyond the normal browser cache.

ScrapIT Agent

ScrapIT Agent runs on a phone or tablet at the yard and connects the yard's devices to the company's ScrapIT server.

Information the app handles

It sends to your company's ScrapIT server only what is needed to run and support the connection: the device's name, the app and Android versions, its local network address, connection status and diagnostic logs, and the print, scale and camera data exchanged with the yard's devices. Bluetooth is used only to send print data to a printer you have paired. The app does not access location, contacts, the camera, the microphone or files.

How information is used

Everything these apps handle is used for one purpose: running your company's operation (tickets, dispatch, shipping, printing and the related records). We do not use this data for advertising or marketing, we do not sell it, we do not share it with data brokers or advertising networks, and the apps contain no advertising, analytics or cross-app tracking.

Retention

Records, including photos, signatures, notes and GPS trails, are retained in your company's ScrapIT system under your company's retention rules. Diagnostic logs are kept only as long as needed for troubleshooting. Uninstalling an app removes what it stored on the device.

Security

Data in transit between the apps and your company's server is encrypted. Access to your company's data requires that company's own server and a sign-in issued by it.

Children

These apps are workplace tools. They are not directed at children and are not intended for use by anyone under the age of 16.
