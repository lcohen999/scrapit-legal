ScrapIT Dispatch — Privacy Policy

IYB Consultants Inc., operating as ScrapIT Software · Effective 25 August 2026

ScrapIT Dispatch is a business tool used by truck drivers at scrap metal, recycling and waste hauling companies to receive and complete the work their dispatch office assigns them. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and who is responsible for it.

Who is responsible for your data

ScrapIT Dispatch is not a consumer service. The app connects to a ScrapIT system operated by or for your employer — the hauling company that issued your driver login. That company decides what data is collected through its dispatch operation and how long it is kept; it is the controller of that data. IYB Consultants Inc. (ScrapIT Software) processes the data on that company's behalf in order to provide the software.

If you are a driver and want work data corrected or deleted, contact your dispatch office — the data lives in your company's ScrapIT system, and your company directs what happens to it. We support those requests when your company passes them to us.

Information the app collects

  • Driver identity. Your driver code and name, as set up by your company, are used to sign you in and to attribute the work you complete.
  • Precise location. While you are signed in, the app reports the vehicle's position so your dispatch office can see where trucks are, measure the distance to each stop, and order the day's work. It also records a GPS trail against each slip so arrival and departure times are accurate. If you grant background location, this continues while the app is not on screen; if you decline, the app remains fully usable and only automatic arrival detection stops.
  • Photos and camera. The camera is used to scan container barcodes and to photograph bins, sites and paperwork. Photos you capture or attach are uploaded to your company's ScrapIT system as part of the slip record.
  • Signatures and notes. Customer signatures captured on screen and any notes you type become part of the slip record in your company's system.
  • Container and bin data. Bin numbers you scan or enter, and the drop, pickup or reject actions you record.
  • Diagnostics. If the app encounters an error, technical details of the error, your device's browser identification, and its network address are reported to the server so problems can be found and fixed. This is used only for troubleshooting.

Storage on your device

ScrapIT Dispatch is built to keep working where trucks lose signal. To do that, it stores a copy of your assigned slips on the device, and while you are offline it queues the actions you take — container logs, photos, signatures, notes — on the device until a connection returns, at which point they sync to your company's system automatically. If you choose "Remember me", your driver code is stored on the device so you can sign in faster. This locally stored work data is a working copy: its permanent home is your company's ScrapIT system, and uninstalling the app removes the local copy.

How information is used

Everything the app collects is used for one purpose: running your company's dispatch operation — assigning work, navigating to sites, recording completed work, and keeping the dispatch office informed. We do not use this data for advertising or marketing, we do not sell it, we do not share it with data brokers or advertising networks, and the app contains no cross-app tracking.

Sharing with service providers

To draw maps and plan routes, the app and its server use mapping services: map imagery is loaded from OpenStreetMap tile servers, and street addresses are converted to map coordinates using a geocoding provider. These providers receive only the technical data needed for the request (such as coordinates, an address to look up, and your device's network address), and route calculations for ordering stops are performed using a directions service. No other third parties receive data from the app, except where the law requires disclosure.

Retention

Slip records — including photos, signatures, notes and GPS trails — are retained in your company's ScrapIT system under your company's retention rules. Diagnostic logs are kept only as long as needed for troubleshooting.

Security

Data in transit between the app and your company's server is encrypted. Access to your company's data requires that company's own server and a driver login issued by it.

Children

ScrapIT Dispatch is a workplace tool for commercial drivers. It is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 16.

Changes to this policy
